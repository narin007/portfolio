High-Speed Sorting System

MULTI-STAGE CONVEYOR · DUAL CHECKWEIGHERS · SIEMENS S7-1200
100 items/min

Problem

Sorting throughput capped the line, and a mechanical redesign at this scale normally means discovering clearance and load faults on the shop floor — after fabrication.

Approach

Specified a multi-stage conveyor line with dual independent checkweighers running in parallel, driven by Siemens S7-1200 PLCs and pneumatic actuators — then validated the whole layout in 3D structural and kinematic simulation before cutting steel.

Result

Sorting throughput raised 400% to 100 items/min. Clearance and load issues caught at design stage, avoiding an estimated ฿1,000,000 in rework.

03

Edge AI Vision Counter

YOLOv8 · CORAL EDGE TPU · RASPBERRY PI 5 · CLOUD LOGGING
80% lower capex

Problem

Product counting was manual — a full hour a day of tallying, with transcription error baked in. Commercial vision systems quoted at several times the budget.

Approach

Trained a YOLOv8 detection model via CiRA-CORE and Google Colab and deployed it on-line on Raspberry Pi 5 with a Google Coral Edge TPU, with results auto-logged to the cloud and surfaced on operator dashboards.

Result

Capital expenditure cut roughly 80% versus vendor systems, 1 hour/day of manual tracking eliminated, and the counting logic stayed in-house to retrain as products change.

05

Non-Blocking Control Architecture

ESP32 · FreeRTOS · STATE MACHINE · FOOD PROCESSING
Real-time safe

Problem

Cloud logging on a food-processing controller competes with the control loop — a blocking transmit means a missed real-time deadline on live equipment.

Approach

Built the controller on ESP32 + FreeRTOS with data transmission isolated as a separate background task, applying state-machine design, interrupt service routines and hardware debouncing; HMI panels integrated with relay boards over Modbus RS485 on an isolated control LAN.

Result

Cloud logging never interrupts real-time loops — the plant gets telemetry without trading away deterministic control.
